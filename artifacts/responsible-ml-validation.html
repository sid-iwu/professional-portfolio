Introduction

In the Workshop 4 Data Challenge Scenarios, I considered how data cleaning, distribution shift and privacy risks can affect an ML project. The workshop summary recommended turning those decisions into a checklist with measurable criteria. I adapted my reasoning into a worksheet an engineering team can use during a review.

Description

The worksheet covers a cleaned dataset, a hiring-model pilot and a federated learning design. Each scenario pairs the evidence to collect with conditions for advancing. The decision log records results, who owns the decision and what to do if a check is on hold. These are proposed checks based on simulations.

Objective

Turn the risks identified in the coursework into checks that a reviewer can measure and document before approving the next step.

Process

  1. Preserved the three workshop scenarios and identified the decision each one asks a team to make.
  2. Translated the original reasoning into evidence requests. For example, the cleaning review compares removal rates, and the hiring review tracks qualified candidates the model misses.
  3. Used the workshop’s follow-up questions to distinguish update exposure from inference risks and added a way to record the remaining risks alongside model utility.

Tools and Technologies Used

I drew on the simulated workshop and its written summary. I used Word-compatible authoring and AI-assisted editing to format and refine the checklist. Google and NIST references support the evaluation and privacy concepts. Federated learning, secure aggregation and differential privacy are techniques evaluated in the scenario.

Value Proposition

Unique Value

The checklist makes each risk actionable. It specifies which measurement a reviewer should request, when the evidence is too weak to proceed and where to record the decision.

Relevance

Engineering leads can use it to structure an ML review and assign follow-up work. It demonstrates how I turn technical risks into decisions that teams can document, a skill I can use when helping organizations adopt reliable AI.

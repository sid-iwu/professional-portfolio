Responsible ML Validation Checklist

Use this checklist to decide whether the evidence supports advancing a dataset, a hiring-model pilot, or a federated learning design. The checks come from three simulated coursework scenarios and describe proposed validation.

Siddharth Jadhav · AIML-500 Workshop 4

Download the Word checklist ↓

Using the checklist

Agree on thresholds, sample sizes and owners before reviewing results. Record the evidence and mark each check Pass, Hold or Not Applicable. Put the decision on hold when a required check fails or the evidence is incomplete.

Data quality and cleaning

Before accepting a cleaned training dataset

  1. Quality and labels

    Evidence to record
    Save a version of the raw data. Record missing values, invalid values and duplicates. Audit a sample of labels and record the disagreement rate.

    When to advance
    Critical issues are resolved and label disagreement meets the agreed limit.

  2. Representation

    Evidence to record
    Compare before-and-after counts and removal rates by group or source, where relevant.

    When to advance
    Investigate any group whose removal rate exceeds the agreed limit. Resolve or document the reason before accepting the dataset.

  3. Data leakage

    Evidence to record
    Count exact duplicates across training and test sets. Check for overlap at the required unit, such as customer ID.

    When to advance
    Zero cross-split duplicates or prohibited entity overlap. Fit learned preprocessing on training data only.

Bias and distribution shift

Before expanding a hiring-model pilot

  1. Local evidence

    Evidence to record
    Test on representative local cases. Record coverage by role or site and compare model performance with the current process using the same cases and independently reviewed, job-relevant labels.

    When to advance
    Local coverage and performance meet agreed criteria. Historical hiring decisions alone are insufficient labels.

  2. Missed candidates

    Evidence to record
    For each approved evaluation group, record qualified candidates missed ÷ all independently assessed qualified candidates. Include counts and uncertainty.

    When to advance
    Each rate and the largest gap meet the agreed limits. Hold where sample sizes are too small to judge.

  3. Pilot controls

    Evidence to record
    Record who reviews decisions, what is monitored and which results trigger a pause.

    When to advance
    Use a shadow pilot where predictions do not drive hiring decisions. Expand only after the review owner signs off.

Privacy and governance

Before advancing a federated learning design

  1. Data compatibility

    Evidence to record
    Compare schema, units, label definitions and coverage across participants. Record mismatches and how they will be resolved.

    When to advance
    Required fields and target definitions align across the participating sites.

  2. Update and model exposure

    Evidence to record
    Document who can access individual updates and the trained model. Test the proposed secure aggregation and access controls, confirm audit logging and assess compromised-participant and inference risks.

    When to advance
    The privacy and security reviewers accept the residual risk for the documented threat model.

  3. Privacy and utility

    Evidence to record
    If using differential privacy, record what unit is protected, the privacy budget and the assumptions used to calculate it. Compare utility with a baseline on the same test set.

    When to advance
    The privacy budget and any loss in utility meet limits agreed before testing.

Decision log

For each check, record the observed result, threshold, evidence link and owner. If a check is on hold, record the next action and review date. Explain any Not Applicable entry. Use the editable table in the Word checklist to document the review.
